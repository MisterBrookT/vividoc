What is an Artificial Neural Network?

Connectionist Architectures and Forward-Propagating Activation Signals.

Artificial sentience relies on staggering parallel connectivity. By chaining hundreds of simple perceptrons into dense hidden layers, simplistic linear algebra transforms into a robust, non-linear predictive engine—capable of carving profound, multidimensional decision boundaries from raw data.
$$ a_k^{(L)} = \sigma \left( \sum_{j} w_{kj} \cdot a_j^{(L-1)} + b_k \right) $$
  • w_kj: The explicit synaptic connection weighting between neurons.
  • σ (Sigma): The non-linear threshold activation function.
  • a_k: The resulting excitatory output of the k-th neuron.
