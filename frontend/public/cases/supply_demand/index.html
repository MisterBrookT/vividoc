Drag the blue D curve up or down to shift demand. Drag the green S curve to shift supply.

Watch the amber dot — the equilibrium point — jump to a new price and quantity as you move each curve.

Price settles where buyers and sellers agree: \(P^* = (a - c)/(b + d)\). Shift demand up and both price and quantity rise; shift supply up and price falls while quantity rises. Every market shock is just a curve moving — the equilibrium follows automatically.

Equilibrium Conditions
\[Q_d = a - bP, \quad Q_s = c + dP\] \[P^* = \frac{a - c}{b + d}, \qquad Q^* = a - bP^*\]
Section 02

Price Elasticity & Total Revenue

Elasticity Explorer Adjust elasticity — watch demand curve & revenue change
Elasticity |ε| 1.00
Regime Unit Elastic

Drag the Elasticity |ε| slider left for inelastic demand (steep curve), right for elastic demand (flat curve).

Watch the right panel: the amber peak on the TR curve marks the price that maximizes revenue — it shifts as elasticity changes.

Raise price on inelastic demand and revenue climbs; raise it on elastic demand and revenue collapses. The TR peak always sits at unit elasticity (\(|\varepsilon| = 1\)). Pharmaceutical pricing exploits inelasticity; airline seat sales exploit elasticity.

Point Elasticity & Revenue Theorem
\[\varepsilon = \frac{dQ}{dP} \cdot \frac{P}{Q}, \qquad \text{TR} = P \cdot Q\] \[\frac{d(\text{TR})}{dP} = Q(1 + \varepsilon) \begin{cases} > 0 & |\varepsilon| < 1 \text{ (inelastic)} \\ = 0 & |\varepsilon| = 1 \text{ (unit elastic, TR max)} \\ < 0 & |\varepsilon| > 1 \text{ (elastic)} \end{cases}\]

Toggle between Free Market, Price Ceiling, and Price Floor.

Watch the amber DWL triangle appear when a control binds — and watch Total Surplus fall below the free-market value.

Any binding price control destroys gains from trade. A ceiling creates shortage and shrinks producer surplus; a floor creates surplus and shrinks consumer surplus. The amber deadweight-loss triangle represents welfare permanently lost — transferred to no one. Free markets exhaust all mutually beneficial trades.

Welfare Measures
\[\text{CS} = \tfrac{1}{2}(P_{\max} - P^*)\,Q^*, \quad \text{PS} = \tfrac{1}{2}(P^* - P_{\min})\,Q^*\] \[\text{DWL} = \tfrac{1}{2}|P_{\text{control}} - P^*| \cdot |Q_{\text{control}} - Q^*|\]
