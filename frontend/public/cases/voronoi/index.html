What are Voronoi Diagrams?

Spatial Partitioning and the Geometry of Nearest Intersecting Neighbors.

Scatter a random collection of seed points across any flat surface, and the plane will silently carve itself into territories. Every region's boundary is defined by exactly one rule: this is the terrain closest to me. From cellular biology to urban planning, Voronoi tessellations emerge wherever nature partitions resources by proximity.
$$ R_k = \{ x \in X \mid d(x, P_k) \le d(x, P_j) \text{ for all } j \neq k \} $$
  • P_k: The designated origin point producing a regional nucleus.
  • R_k: The resulting convex spatial tessellation boundary.
  • Distance metric: Euclidean triangulation isolating the absolute minimal trajectory.
