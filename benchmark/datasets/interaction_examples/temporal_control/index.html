What is a Fourier Series?

Deconstructing Periodic Waveforms into Infinite Sinusoidal Epicycles.

Jean-Baptiste Joseph Fourier proved a profound mathematical truth: the most rigid, jagged, and discontinuous signals in the universe are merely illusions. Beneath their sharp edges lies an infinite chorus of perfectly smooth, continuous sinusoidal waves oscillating together in intricate harmonic resonance.
$$ f(t) = \frac{4}{\pi} \sum_{n=1,3,5\dots}^{\infty} \frac{1}{n} \sin(n \omega t) $$
  • n: The odd harmonic scaling integer defining the epicycle tier.
  • ω (Omega): The fundamental rotational velocity.
  • T (Time): Scrubbing the temporal parameter sweeps the drawing vector.
