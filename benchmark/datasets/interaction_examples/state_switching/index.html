What are Electron Orbitals?

Quantum Superposition and Atomic Probability Densities.

The planetary model of atoms circling nuclei like predictable moons is a classical illusion. In the quantum realm, electrons blur into probabilistic smears. By evaluating Hamiltonian wavefunctions, we don't find a precise particle bullet, but rather beautiful, ethereal clouds of spatial statistical likelihood.
$$ \hat{H}\Psi = E\Psi \implies |\Psi_{n,l,m}(\mathbf{r})|^2 = \text{Probability Density} $$
  • 1s (Spherical): The fundamental, boundary-less symmetrical cloud anchoring the nucleus.
  • 2p (Dumbbell): Introducing angular momentum forces electron density away from the planar node.
  • 3d (Clover): High energy subshells producing intricate, multi-lobar spatial distributions.
